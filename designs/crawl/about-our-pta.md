### Who We Are:
A nonprofit organization composed of parents, families, and teachers dedicated to supporting Adams Elementary.
### WHAT WE DO:
Support our students, staff and community through volunteer efforts, fundraising, advocacy and other supports.
### WHY PTA:
Our Parent Teacher Association is made up of members who are part of a greater network that includes the Seattle Council PTSA, Washington State PTA and National PTA. Our members elect an executive board, vote on our budget and other issues, and uphold standards set by the city, state and national Parent Teacher Associations. While our mission is to support Adams Elementary, we are an independent organization.

### Volunteer! 
We need all kinds of volunteers, from helping at school events to fulfilling a one-time or continuing PTA role. Visit our Volunteer page and also start the process of registering with SPS as a volunteer
### Attend a Meeting
You do not need to be a PTA member or hold a board member role to attend our monthly board meetings or any of our three general meetings of the year. Come meet other volunteers and learn about what we do. Follow our Calendar and Konstella updates.
### Attend a Community Event
For our events to be a success, we want our families to attend! Watch out for communication about our many activities like Fall Fest, Multicultural Potluck, Talent Fest and other fun events throughout the year.
