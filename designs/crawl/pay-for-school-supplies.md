## CONTRIBUTE TO SCHOOL SUPPLIES
The Adams Elementary PTA is proud to arrange the purchase and distribution of our school supplies for all our students. This service is very popular with teachers and families! 
​
Families have overwhelmingly requested that they chip in for these supplies rather than have valuable PTA funding redirected to these items. So we provide this opportunity to do so. Teachers are very grateful for this system.​
Contribute Now
If paying for supplies is a hardship for you, you may request a scholarship. No personal information is required.
