#### A few of our Adams Teachers regularly host Parents Night Out. For a fee you can drop your students off for a fun night IN at Adams! There is a PNO almost every month and they typically are on Friday nights from 6-9. 
### Stay Tuned for Dates!
Bell Time
7:35: Supervision begins on the playground;  breakfast service begins in the lunchroom 
7:50: First bell (call to classrooms) 
​
7:55: Instruction begins
​
2:25: End of instruction
1:10: Early dismissal on Wednesdays
