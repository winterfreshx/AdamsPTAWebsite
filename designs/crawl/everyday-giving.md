Your school involvement matters! All donations made to the Adams PTA (rather directly or through the listed donation programs below) directly supports the students, teachers and staff at Adams Elementary. Contributing your support to Adams does not need to equate to a lot of time or money. These are the many small everyday ways you can give your support. 
#### EVERYDAY WAYS TO GIVE
#### Donate Directly To The Adams PTA
The Adams PTA excepts donations of all sizes (cash, check or by credit card) all year round. 
Adams PTA is a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Your donation is 100% tax deductible. Donate today!
#### Box Tops For Education
The Box Tops program is now digital-only. If you see this label (pictured to the right), use the new Box Tops app to scan your receipt. 
Box Tops are still worth 10¢ each for your school. The app will find participating products purchased at any store and instantly add cash to your school's earnings online. Learn more and download the app Here
#### Fred Meyer
Link your Fred Meyer Rewards Card to Adams PTA! Use code 91334 or search for Adams Elementary in the Community Rewards enrollment section. You will still continue to get your own rewards points, fuel points and rebates. Cards available in store.
#### Smith Brothers! 
Sign up for home grocery delivery service with the Adams code ADAMSPTA and you will receive $15 off your 1st delivery and Adams will receive a $50 donation. 
Follow this link: smithbrothersfarms.com/?dc=adamspta
