### Adams Corporate Matching
Many employers will match your donation and/or your volunteer hours, which is an easy way to secure more resources for Adams! Take these simple steps:
Confirm eligibility with your Human Resources team and complete the necessary forms
Make your gift of time (submit your volunteer hours through Benevity or whatever tool your employer uses to track) or treasure (financial contribution flagged for your employer as a match) to Adams Elementary PTA – Tax ID: 91-0963029
Watch the impact of your dollars and hours support our school!
Many companies allow employees to seek a match for donations made within the last year – it may not be too late to get a match for an earlier contribution to Adams! If you need support or more information around how to take advantage of corporate matching, please reach out to ptatreasurer@adamselementary.org.
