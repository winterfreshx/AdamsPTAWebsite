### Become a Member of the Adams PTA Today!
By joining the Adams PTA, you are not only supporting our school, but becoming a member of  a larger network of state and national PTA organizations. Memberships begin on July 1st and are valid through June 30.
​
Select the desired membership level below and this will take you to join and pay in WSPTA's member database at Givebacks.com.

## WHAT IS GIVEBACKS ?
Why does the membership link look different? Thanks to a new membership platform adopted by the WSPTA, we are now asking members to join directly in the member database on Givebacks.com. By joining through the links above you are saving hundreds of volunteer hours that it would take to manually enter all members into the secure state database. You will also get the opportunity to connect your membership account to a fundraising system that earns money at selected retailers and even local businesses. You will receive a digital WSPTA membership card when you join.
​
WSPTA began using Givebacks in 2023/2024. Members should feel confident that their personal details are secure, and that the system is a positive change for PTAs in Washington State with its additional features, including fundraising options. This system is new to Adams PTA, and while we are excited to learn it we appreciate your patience with any hiccups.
