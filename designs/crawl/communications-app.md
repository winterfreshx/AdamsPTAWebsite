## Join Us On Konstella
Konstella is a private online parent community. Download our app or use the web interface. 
​
Most of our timely communications and interactive technology will be showing up in Konstella. You are not required to join, but setting up your account gives you the most options to control how you receive notifications from the PTA (whether its email, push notifications or both).
​
On Konstella you can also find our directory, connect with PTA leaders, volunteer, send private messages to other parents, and more. 
​
Join today at www.konstella.com and click "find your school," or look out for invitations from PTA or your teachers.
